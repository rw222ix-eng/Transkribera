=== BÖRJAN PÅ BASPROMPT A ===
PAINTING STYLE: thick painterly gouache with visible brush strokes
and canvas tooth, no line art, no cel shading, no outlines, no smooth digital
gradients, no vector look. A deep cobalt-blue sky with a real tonal range, darker at
the top edge and lighter toward the horizon. Enormous sculptural cumulus towers
built from many rounded lobes, cool grey-blue shaded undersides and cream-white
sunlit crowns. Ground painted with visible individual grass blades and small flower
dabs, layered yellow-green over deep green, with strong dark cast shadows. No
atmospheric haze and no pale distant mountains — the horizon is a simple dark band.
Human figures are painted as people, with faces. Paint the face readably wherever a
figure is close enough to show one.

WHAT IS FIXED AND WHAT THE SCENE MAY OVERRIDE. Fixed in every plate: thick gouache
with visible brush strokes, a real tonal range rather than flat fill, warm
cream-white where the light lands and a cool complementary tone in shadow, no
atmospheric haze, no pale distant mountain ranges, and no line art. Faces are
painted. The scene below MAY override the time of day, the season and the palette —
dusk, night, autumn, winter, rain — and when it does, that overrides the summer sky
and the cumulus towers described above. Follow the scene.

THIS IS A BACKGROUND PLATE, NOT A FINISHED FIGURE. It is one frame of a 16:9
educational animation, and the mathematical notation will be drawn on top of it
later by a separate program. Therefore:

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT. No letters, no words, no numbers, no digits, no mathematical
symbols, no equations, no labels, no captions, no signature, no watermark anywhere
in the image. No drawn measurement lines, no arrows, no dashed lines, no angle arcs,
no coordinate axes, no grid, no circles marking points, no rulers, no annotation of
any kind. Nothing that looks drawn on top of the painting. Only paint.

DRAWABILITY. Notation — measurement arrows, angle arcs, tangents, pointers — will be
drawn on top of this painting by a separate program, from coordinates measured in the
finished image. The painting must therefore make its own geometry findable:

* ONE clear subject. Not a group, not a cluster, not a repeated pattern, unless the
  scene explicitly asks for a countable row.
* The subject is shown in clean side view or straight-on, never three-quarter and
  never at a rakish angle. A measurable object is a flat object.
* The whole subject is inside the frame with air around it. Nothing important is
  cropped by an edge and nothing overlaps the subject.
* Both ends of the subject are visible and unobstructed: where it meets the ground
  and where it ends against the sky. Do not hide the base in grass, do not hide the
  top behind a cloud.
* The subject's silhouette meets a CALM surface — plain sky, plain water, plain
  field. Do not place a cloud bank, a treeline or a busy horizon directly behind the
  subject's edges.
* Vertical things are vertical and horizontal things are horizontal. No leaning, no
  tilted horizon, no dutch angle.
* The ground line under the subject is visible and continuous, not broken by bushes,
  rocks or dips.
* If the scene compares two things, both stand on the same visible ground line, both
  are fully visible, and their sizes differ clearly.
* If the scene asks for a row of repeated objects, there are at most twelve, they are
  evenly spaced, and the four nearest are clearly separated from each other.
* Any angle that the scene exists to show must read as between 20 and 60 degrees in
  the finished picture. A 5-degree slope is a straight line and cannot be marked.
* Leave one large continuous quiet region, at least a third of the frame, with no
  clouds, no flowers and no high-contrast detail. Notation goes there.
* The subject sits in the middle or the right half. The upper left sixth and the
  upper right corner stay plain.
* For a top-down or aerial scene: straight edges stay straight, corners of fields
  and shorelines stay visible and unrounded, and no object sits on a corner that
  will need to be measured.
* Include a human figure next to the subject when the scene needs scale, standing
  clear of the subject with space between them, its face painted readably.

COMPOSITION RULES, they matter as much as the subject:
- Full bleed 16:9, no border, no frame, no device mockup, no vignette darkening.
- The upper left sixth of the frame is plain sky or plain dark paint — a handwritten
  title will be placed there. The upper right corner and the bottom centre stay
  equally plain, for a step indicator and a caption.
- The horizon, when there is one, sits low, between 70 and 80 percent of the frame
  height, so the sky dominates.
