PAINTING STYLE: The frame itself is a
sheet of paper.

THE SHEET. A sheet of warm off-white paper fills the frame edge to edge, painted in
gouache: visible paper grain and fibre, faint cool grey-blue paint shading along the
edges and in the corners, a few soft painterly stains, one barely visible horizontal
fold across the lower third. It reads as a real sheet that has been painted, not as
a flat digital background. Slightly cooler and bluer at the very edges, warmest in
the middle.

THE TAPED PAINTING. One small rectangular gouache painting, roughly the size of a
postcard and about a quarter of the frame wide, is taped onto the sheet, rotated a
couple of degrees, held by two hand-painted strips of pale masking tape, with a soft
painted shadow underneath it. That little painting is the only saturated colour in
the whole image, and it carries the reference style at full richness: deep cobalt
sky, cumulus lobes with cream-white sunlit crowns and grey-blue undersides, deep
yellow-green meadow, thick visible brush strokes. Its motif is simple and readable
at postcard size — one clear subject, seen from the side, against calm sky. Its motif
and its position on the sheet are given below.

THIS IS A BACKGROUND PLATE, NOT A FINISHED PAGE. It is one frame of a 16:9
educational animation, and every pencil line, every axis, every curve, every digit
and every handwritten word will be drawn on top of it later by a separate program.
Therefore:

THE SHEET IS COMPLETELY BLANK. No writing, no handwriting, no letters, no words, no
numbers, no digits, no mathematical symbols. No pencil lines, no ruled lines, no
grid, no squares, no coordinate axes, no curves, no arrows, no sketches, no doodles,
no margin line, no holes, no staples, no signature, no watermark. Nothing at all is
drawn on the paper. Only painted paper and the one taped painting.

DRAWABILITY. A coordinate system, several rows of handwriting and a full calculation
will be drawn onto the empty paper by a separate program, from coordinates measured
in the finished image. Therefore the empty region must be genuinely usable:

* At least two thirds of the sheet is one single continuous empty area, not several
  smaller gaps separated by stains or by the vignette.
* That area is evenly lit and even in tone. No stain, no fold, no shading gradient
  and no tape crosses it.
* The paper texture there is fine and low in contrast. A coarse mottled surface
  makes thin pencil lines unreadable.
* The vignette sits fully inside one corner with its tape, and nothing about it
  reaches into the empty area.

COMPOSITION RULES:
- Full bleed 16:9, no border, no frame, no device mockup, no desk, no hands, no
  pencils lying on the sheet. The sheet IS the frame; its edges are not visible.
- The bottom centre stays clean for a step indicator.
